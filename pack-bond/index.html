I've spent over a decade doing what most trainers only talk about — getting into the mind of the dog. Not the obedient version owners want, but the actual animal: curious, instinct-driven, relationship-seeking, and capable of far more than most people believe.

My journey deepened inside the world's largest doggy daycare in Dubai, where managing hundreds of dogs daily meant reading behaviour at speed, solving problems under pressure, and learning what actually works — not what looks good in a training video.

For the past 7+ years, I've taken groups of up to 8 dogs on 2+ hour off-leash hikes. No fences. No shortcuts. Just trust, communication, and a methodology built on understanding how dogs think, not just how to make them comply.

My 13-year-old Giant Alaskan Malamute has been my greatest teacher — and the living proof that a lifetime of connection, not correction, creates a dog you'll never stop being proud of.

I work with puppies, reactive dogs, frustrated walkers, and everything in between. Whether you want the technical deep-dive or the plain-English version — I speak both.

Psychology First
Every behaviour has a reason. We find it, understand it, and work with it — not against it. No suppression, no shortcuts.
Owner Education
You are the constant in your dog's life. My job is to give you the knowledge to lead them confidently, every day.
Real World Training
Training that works in your home, on your street, at the park, and everywhere life takes you and your dog.
Pack Community
You don't just hire a trainer — you join a pack. A community of owners who share the same commitment to their dogs.
