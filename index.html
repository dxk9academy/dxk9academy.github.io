Why most training doesn't work

Most training fails before it starts. Not because of the dog — because of how the problem is approached.

01

Wrong environment

Training happens at a facility. The dog behaves there. It comes home and nothing transfers. Because behaviour is context-dependent, and the context was wrong from the start.

02

No system

Generic programmes treat every dog the same. No diagnosis. No prescription. No clear standard for what success looks like. Just sessions — with no defined exit.

03

Owner is left out

The trainer trains the dog. The owner watches. Nothing is transferred. The trainer leaves and the behaviour falls apart within a week because the owner was never part of the system.

Who this is for

Three types of Dubai dog owners. One outcome — a dog you can actually live with.

Profile 01

Problem owners

You have a behaviour problem. You may have tried something already. You need a trainer who will actually diagnose what is happening and fix it — not run generic sessions until you give up.

Profile 02

New owners

You have a puppy or a new dog and you want to start correctly. You understand that the first few months define the next decade. You want structure from day one, not damage control later.

Profile 03

Urban lifestyle owners

Your dog doesn't have a behaviour problem. You want a dog that fits Dubai city life — the apartment, the lobby, the lift, the weekend walk. Calm. Reliable. Enjoyable to live with.
