Common questions

Is the Discovery Call really free?

Yes. The Discovery Call is a 15-minute conversation at no charge. There is no commitment required. If Dexter determines that your case is a good fit, the next step is a paid Consultation Visit.

What should I prepare before the call?

Nothing formal. Be ready to describe your dog — age, breed, the specific behaviours you're dealing with, and what you've tried. Honest information leads to better outcomes.

What happens after the call?

If your case is a good fit, Dexter will schedule a Consultation Visit. This is an in-home assessment where he observes the dog in its actual environment and produces a written training plan. The Consultation Visit costs 350 AED + 5% VAT.

Where do sessions take place?

All sessions are in-home. Dexter comes to you. Sessions take place in your apartment, your building, and your immediate environment — the lobby, the lift, the street. Training happens where the behaviour actually occurs.

What areas of Dubai do you cover?

All areas of Dubai. No travel fee within Dubai. Out-of-emirate sessions are considered case by case.

My dog is aggressive. Can you help?

Possibly. Dexter takes aggression cases and assesses each one individually. Some cases are accepted and prescribed the Transformation Programme. Some are referred where a medical component is involved. Book a Discovery Call — that is the right place to start.
