The Programmes

Prescribed.
Not chosen.

Every programme is prescribed following a paid consultation. Clients do not select from a menu. Dexter assesses the situation and determines what the dog needs.

Prices are never discussed until after the consultation. If you are looking for a price list, this is not that. If you want to understand what a programme involves and whether it is right for your dog — read on, then book a Discovery Call.

Puppies — Non-Negotiable

Puppy Foundation Package

8 sessions over 4 weeks — 2 sessions per week

The only fixed, standalone package in the DX K9 Academy system. Every puppy case is automatically prescribed this programme — no exceptions and no alternatives. The Puppy Foundation Package addresses foundation behaviours, socialisation, impulse control, crate acceptance, household structure, and owner education. It is structured this way because the developmental window for a puppy is finite. The work done in the first weeks defines what you are managing for the next decade.

All puppies are automatically prescribed this programme. If your dog is under 6 months, this is the starting point. No other package is available for puppies.

Foundation Cases

Foundation Programme

6 sessions

Prescribed for dogs with basic obedience gaps and mild household management issues. The dog understands some things but the foundation is incomplete or inconsistent. Six sessions to install the structure that should have been there from the beginning.

Adolescent & Moderate Cases

Structure Programme

10 sessions

Prescribed for adolescent dogs, leash issues, early-stage reactivity, and impulse control deficits. The dog has learned some things but the structure is not holding in real-world environments. Ten sessions to build reliability beyond the home.

Complex & Rehabilitation Cases

Transformation Programme

15 sessions

Prescribed for complex behaviour cases, reactivity rehabilitation, fear and anxiety, and aggression (case by case, assessed at consultation). The most intensive programme in the system. Fifteen sessions to systematically rebuild the dog's responses from the ground up.

Available after Structure or Transformation

Real World Proofing

Minimum 6 sessions

For dogs that have completed a structured programme and need their skills proofed in real-world Dubai environments — crowded areas, malls, public transport zones, high-distraction streets. Available only after the Structure or Transformation Programme. Not a standalone entry point.

Graduated Clients Only

Continuity & Mastery Packs

Continuity: 4 sessions / Mastery: 8 sessions

For clients whose dog has graduated — confirmed by Dexter at the 85% real-world reliability standard. Used for maintenance, advancing existing skills, or addressing new challenges that develop after graduation. Not available to dogs that have not completed and graduated from a training programme.

On aggression cases

Dexter takes aggression cases. Every case is assessed individually at the consultation. Some cases are accepted and prescribed the Transformation Programme. Some are referred to a veterinary behaviourist where the root cause has a medical component.

If your dog has bitten, shown aggression toward people or other animals, or you have been advised that the behaviour has a medical component — book a Discovery Call. The correct starting point is an honest conversation, not a guess.
