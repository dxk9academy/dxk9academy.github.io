The Process

How It Works

DX K9 Academy operates like a clinic. Clients do not browse programmes and choose one. Dexter assesses the situation, diagnoses the problem, and prescribes the right course of action.

The clinic model

When you see a specialist, you don't walk in and tell them what procedure you want. You describe the problem. They assess it. They prescribe the solution.

DX K9 Academy works the same way. You describe the situation. Dexter comes to your home and assesses it in person. He identifies the root cause — which is rarely what it appears to be on the surface — and prescribes the exact programme your dog needs.

This matters because a dog that pulls on the leash out of frustration needs a different approach than a dog that pulls out of fear. The behaviour looks identical. The cause — and the solution — are not. A menu of programmes can't tell the difference. A consultation can.

The client journey

Six stages. Every client goes through the same process. Nothing is skipped.

1

Free — 15 minutes

Discovery Call

A short conversation. Dexter asks about your dog, the situation, and what you're dealing with. He assesses whether DX K9 Academy is the right fit for your case. If it is, a Consultation Visit is scheduled. No commitment at this stage.

2

In-Home — 60 minutes — 350 AED + VAT

Consultation Visit

Dexter comes to your home. He observes the dog in its actual environment — the space it lives in, the routines it follows, the people it interacts with. He assesses behaviour, identifies root causes, and delivers a written training plan prescribing exactly what needs to happen and why. The consultation fee is non-refundable. If you proceed with a programme within 48 hours, it is credited toward your package.

3

Prescribed — delivered after consultation

Training Plan

A written document outlining the diagnosis, the prescribed programme, what will be addressed, in what order, and what the exit standard looks like. This is not a generic template — it is specific to your dog, your household, and your situation. Full payment is required before Session 1.

4

In-Home — 60 minutes per session

Training Sessions

Sessions happen in your home and immediate environment — the lobby, the street, the lift, wherever the behaviour actually occurs. Every session ends with Dexter demonstrating your homework. You repeat it in front of him before he leaves. You are part of the programme. After every session, a written Session Recap is sent covering what was covered, the dog's progress, and what you need to do before the next session.

5

Trainer-confirmed — 85% real-world reliability

Graduation

Graduation is confirmed by Dexter when the dog demonstrates 85% reliability in real-world conditions — in the home, in the lobby, on the street, in the environments you actually use. Not in a controlled training session. Graduation is never automatic. It is earned and confirmed. You receive a formal Graduation Summary documenting what was achieved and what your dog's baseline now is.

6

For graduated clients — for life

Lifetime Support

Graduated clients have access to two 15-minute consultations per year, via email, at no charge. For life. For new dogs, a new Discovery Call begins the process again — previous graduation does not transfer to a different animal.

What to expect in a session

Session structure

60 minutes. Every time.

Session 1 is execution, not observation. Dexter works with the dog and the owner from the first minute. There is no extended assessment period once training begins — that happened at the consultation.

Your role

You are part of every session

Dexter does not train the dog while you watch. You are involved from the start. Homework is demonstrated before Dexter leaves — not described, demonstrated. You repeat it in front of him. If it's not right, it's corrected before he goes.

Between sessions

The work happens at home

Minimum 10–15 minutes of practice per day. Every day. Every person in the household applies the same rules, the same markers, the same responses. Consistency is not a preference — it is the mechanism. Without it, the programme produces nothing.
